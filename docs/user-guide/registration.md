# Registering for Coolest Projects

**Audience:** participants (and the parent/guardian helping a younger participant register).

> Draft: written from the app's page structure (`apps/registration/pages/`), not verified against a live event. Please correct anything that doesn't match what you see on the actual site.

## Before you start

1. Open the registration site for the event. The home page shows the event's rules and whether registration is currently open.
2. Read and accept the rules — you won't be able to continue until you do.

## Joining or starting a project

Once registration is open, you have two paths:

- **You already have a voucher/token from a teammate** who started the project — enter it to join their project.
- **You're starting a new project** (on your own or as the team lead) — you'll create it instead.

Either way, you'll first fill in your own details (name, contact info, t-shirt size, and — if you're linked to a CoderDojo — which one).

## If you're creating a new project

- Give your project a name and a short description.
- You can add teammates later; they join using the voucher your project generates.

## Uploading a photo

Projects can attach a photo/attachment as part of registration — this can usually be added or changed later, not just during the initial signup.

## Finishing up

Once your details, project, and (if applicable) team are all set, you'll land on a confirmation page. Keep an eye on your email for confirmation and any follow-up from the organizers.

## If something goes wrong

The registration site shows a clear error message if the event isn't accepting registrations yet, or if the site can't reach its backend — if you see either, try again later or contact the organizers (`info@coolestprojects.be`).
